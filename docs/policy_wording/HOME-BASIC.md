# HOME-BASIC Home Insurance: Policy Wording

## 1. About this policy

This document is the policy wording for the HOME-BASIC home insurance product. It explains what
HOME-BASIC covers, the limits and deductibles that apply, what is excluded and the conditions
the policyholder must meet. It must be read together with the policy schedule, which shows the
policy number, the policyholder, the start date and the end date.

HOME-BASIC is the entry-level product. It covers fire, theft and storm only. It does not cover
water damage or accidental damage, which are available on HOME-STD and HOME-PLUS.

## 2. Definitions

These words have the same meaning wherever they appear in the HOME-BASIC policy wording.

- **We, us, our**: the insurer.
- **You, your**: the policyholder named in the schedule.
- **Home**: the private dwelling at the address in the schedule, including its fixtures and
  fittings, and the contents kept in it.
- **Period of cover**: from the start date up to, but not including, the end date shown in the
  schedule. Each policy runs for 365 days.
- **Loss**: the cost of repairing or replacing what was damaged, lost or stolen, in euro.
- **Limit**: the most we will pay for one claim under a peril.
- **Deductible** (also called the **excess**): the first part of every claim, which you pay
  yourself.
- **Sudden**: happening at a single, identifiable moment, rather than building up over time.
- **Gradual**: happening slowly, over a period of weeks, months or longer.

## 3. Schedule of cover

HOME-BASIC covers the following perils. Limits and deductibles apply **per claim**.

| Peril | Peril code | Limit | Deductible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | `fire` | €150,000 | €500 |
| Theft | `theft` | €10,000 | €250 |
| Storm | `storm` | €50,000 | €500 |
| Water damage | `water_damage` | Not covered | Not covered |
| Accidental damage | `accidental_damage` | Not covered | Not covered |

The sum insured of a HOME-BASIC policy is €150,000, which is the same as the fire limit.

## 4. Fire cover

Under HOME-BASIC we cover loss or damage to your home caused by fire, lightning, explosion or
smoke, up to €150,000 per claim, minus a deductible of €500.

This includes smoke damage from a sudden fire in the home, such as a kitchen fire.

We do not cover:

- scorching, melting or burning of an item that was not caused by a fire spreading beyond it
  (for example, a cigarette burn on a carpet);
- smoke damage that builds up gradually, for example from a stove or chimney over time.

## 5. Theft cover

Under HOME-BASIC we cover theft, or attempted theft, of contents from your home, and damage
done to the home during a theft or attempted theft, up to €10,000 per claim, minus a deductible
of €250.

You must report the theft to An Garda Síochána as soon as possible and give us the Garda
report number.

We do not cover:

- theft by a person who lives in your home or was lawfully allowed into it;
- loss of items that you cannot account for and where there is no sign of theft (unexplained
  disappearance).

## 6. Storm cover

Under HOME-BASIC we cover damage to your home caused by storm, including high winds, heavy
rain, hail and heavy snow that happen with a storm, up to €50,000 per claim, minus a deductible
of €500.

Examples: roof slates or tiles blown off, rain entering through storm-damaged openings, trees
falling onto the home.

We do not cover:

- damage caused by rising water from rivers, lakes or the sea (flood);
- damage that was caused by wear and tear or poor maintenance and only showed up during a storm
  (see section 10.2).

## 7. Water damage cover

Water damage is **not covered** under HOME-BASIC. This applies to every escape of water,
whether sudden (such as a burst pipe or a split washing machine hose) or gradual (such as a
slow leak). A claim for water damage is not payable under this policy.

Water damage cover is available on HOME-STD and HOME-PLUS.

## 8. Accidental damage cover

Accidental damage is **not covered** under HOME-BASIC. A claim for accidental damage, such as
dropping a television or spilling paint on a carpet, is not payable under this policy.
Accidental damage cover is only available on HOME-PLUS.

## 9. How we settle claims

For every claim under HOME-BASIC, we work out the payment like this:

1. Take the loss amount.
2. If the loss is more than the limit for the peril, use the limit instead.
3. Subtract the deductible for the peril.
4. If the result is zero or less, we pay nothing.

In short: **payment = the lower of the loss and the limit, minus the deductible, and never less
than zero**.

Example: a storm loss of €3,000 under HOME-BASIC. The limit is €50,000, so the full €3,000
counts. The deductible is €500, so we pay €2,500.

Example: a theft loss of €12,500 under HOME-BASIC. The limit is €10,000, so only €10,000
counts. The deductible is €250, so we pay €9,750.

Only one deductible applies per claim. The limit applies per claim, not per year.

## 10. Exclusions

The exclusions in this section apply to **every peril** covered by HOME-BASIC. If an exclusion
applies, we do not pay the claim, even if the peril is shown as covered in the schedule.

### 10.1 Gradual damage

We do not cover loss or damage that happens gradually, over a period of time, rather than
suddenly. This includes:

- damp, rot, mould, mildew or condensation;
- damage that you knew about, or should reasonably have noticed, and did not fix.

### 10.2 Wear and tear and lack of maintenance

We do not cover wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or damage caused by not keeping the home
in a reasonable state of repair.

### 10.3 Deliberate acts

We do not cover loss or damage caused on purpose by you or by anyone living in your home.

### 10.4 Perils not shown in the schedule

We do not cover loss or damage caused by a peril that is not shown as covered in section 3.
Under HOME-BASIC this includes water damage and accidental damage.

### 10.5 General exclusions

We do not cover loss or damage caused by war, terrorism, nuclear risks, pollution or
contamination, or by pests and vermin.

## 11. Conditions

### 11.1 Period of cover

We only cover loss or damage that happens during the period of cover: on or after the start
date and before the end date shown in the schedule. A loss that happens after the end date is
not covered unless the policy has been renewed.

If the policy is cancelled, cover ends on the cancellation date. A loss that happens on or
after the cancellation date is not covered.

### 11.2 Making a claim

You must tell us about a claim as soon as possible and give us:

- the policy number;
- the date the loss happened;
- what happened and which peril caused it;
- the amount you are claiming, with quotes, invoices or receipts;
- a Garda report number for theft.

We may ask for more information before we can make a decision.

### 11.3 Honesty

If a claim is fraudulent or exaggerated, we will not pay it and we may cancel the policy.
